# Consultant Quotes Compilation (All Assistant Outputs)

## Several ways to attack this as a quote (2–3 sentences each)

### Certification ≠ competence
“Too many consultants treat certifications like experience. They can tell you *what* a framework is, but not *how* to make it work—or *why* it matters. If the deliverable is just diagrams, you didn’t hire change; you hired noise.”

### Diagrams don’t do the work
“Diagrams are not transformation—they’re just expensive origami. If a consultant can’t translate boxes and arrows into new decisions, behaviors, and outcomes, they’re not creating clarity. They’re adding to the clutter.”

### What vs. how vs. why
“A lot of consultants are fluent in the *what* and allergic to the *how* and *why*. They’ll map your org perfectly and still leave it exactly the same. When the work ends at the slide deck, the only thing that changes is the file name.”

### Real-world scars
“Certifications are clean; real projects are messy. I trust the people who’ve wrestled change into reality, not the ones who’ve memorized the labels. If all you get is a diagram, you bought vocabulary—not progress.”

### Framework theater
“Some consultants do framework theater: big on credentials, light on consequences. They’ll produce beautiful diagrams that never survive contact with Monday morning. Clarity isn’t a graphic—it’s changed behavior.”

### Call-out with a punchline
“If your consultant’s main artifact is a diagram, congratulations—you’ve purchased decorative bureaucracy. The point isn’t to describe the organization; it’s to change it. Otherwise, you’re just paying someone to narrate the obvious.”

### Quietly brutal
“Credentials are easy to display; outcomes are harder to produce. If the work stops at diagrams, it doesn’t clarify anything—it competes for attention. Real consulting ends in change, not shapes.”
