# Clinical Workflow Hierarchical Classification

## 1. Information Management
### 1.1 Reviewing
- Reading charts, labs, imaging
- Examining historical data
- Checking documentation
- Studying trends

### 1.2 Documenting
- Creating clinical notes
- Recording procedures
- Writing orders
- Maintaining records

### 1.3 Acknowledging
- Signing off results
- Confirming receipts
- Verifying completions
- Accepting handoffs

## 2. Clinical Decision-Making
### 2.1 Evaluating
- Assessing patient status
- Analyzing treatment responses
- Determining interventions
- Judging effectiveness

### 2.2 Planning
- Developing treatment strategies
- Creating care plans
- Setting clinical goals
- Designing interventions

### 2.3 Ordering
- Requesting tests
- Prescribing medications
- Ordering procedures
- Initiating protocols

## 3. Care Delivery
### 3.1 Carrying Out
- Performing procedures
- Administering treatments
- Conducting examinations
- Executing interventions

### 3.2 Monitoring
- Tracking vital signs
- Following symptoms
- Observing responses
- Surveillance activities

## 4. Care Coordination
### 4.1 Communicating
- Patient education
- Team discussions
- Family updates
- Handoff activities

### 4.2 Coordinating
- Managing transitions
- Arranging services
- Orchestrating care
- Resource allocation

## Workflow Examples Showing Hierarchical Relationships:

### Example 1: Managing New Admission
1. Information Management
   - Reviewing: Check ED notes
   - Documenting: Write admission note
   - Acknowledging: Accept handoff

2. Clinical Decision-Making
   - Evaluating: Assess current status
   - Planning: Develop treatment plan
   - Ordering: Initial admission orders

3. Care Delivery
   - Carrying Out: Initial examination
   - Monitoring: Track response

4. Care Coordination
   - Communicating: Update family
   - Coordinating: Arrange consultations

### Example 2: Managing Acute Change
1. Information Management
   - Reviewing: Check vital signs
   - Documenting: Update notes
   - Acknowledging: Critical lab values

2. Clinical Decision-Making
   - Evaluating: Assess urgency
   - Planning: Create response plan
   - Ordering: Stat orders

3. Care Delivery
   - Carrying Out: Immediate interventions
   - Monitoring: Frequent reassessment

4. Care Coordination
   - Communicating: Rapid response team
   - Coordinating: Emergency resources

## Key Benefits of Hierarchical Structure:

1. Logical Grouping
- Related activities are clustered
- Clear parent-child relationships
- Natural workflow progression

2. Process Understanding
- Shows activity relationships
- Highlights dependencies
- Reveals workflow patterns

3. System Design Applications
- EMR menu organization
- Interface layout
- Workflow optimization

4. Training Benefits
- Structured learning approach
- Clear skill progression
- Role-based organization
