# Clinical Workflow Categories: Task Classification System

## 1. Reviewing
- Reading patient charts and history
- Examining lab results and diagnostic tests
- Analyzing imaging studies
- Checking vital signs trends
- Reading nursing notes and documentation
- Reviewing medication lists and histories
- Examining previous care plans

## 2. Ordering
- Requesting diagnostic tests
- Prescribing medications
- Ordering medical imaging
- Requesting specialist consultations
- Ordering nursing interventions
- Scheduling procedures
- Creating dietary orders

## 3. Documenting
- Writing progress notes
- Creating admission notes
- Completing discharge summaries
- Recording procedures
- Writing prescription details
- Documenting patient instructions
- Creating care plans
- Recording patient assessments

## 4. Acknowledging
- Signing off on test results
- Confirming medication administration
- Verifying completed orders
- Accepting patient handoffs
- Acknowledging critical alerts
- Confirming receipt of consultations
- Signing procedural consent forms

## 5. Carrying Out
- Performing physical examinations
- Administering medications
- Conducting procedures
- Providing direct patient care
- Implementing treatment plans
- Performing wound care
- Executing nursing interventions

## 6. Monitoring
- Tracking vital signs
- Observing patient symptoms
- Following disease progression
- Watching for medication effects
- Monitoring treatment responses
- Surveillance for complications
- Tracking care plan progress

## 7. Planning
- Developing treatment strategies
- Creating care plans
- Setting clinical goals
- Planning discharge processes
- Designing intervention schedules
- Establishing monitoring parameters
- Creating contingency plans

## 8. Communicating
- Conducting patient education
- Performing team handoffs
- Leading family discussions
- Participating in rounds
- Providing updates to colleagues
- Conducting team meetings
- Managing patient inquiries

## 9. Coordinating
- Managing care transitions
- Arranging specialist consultations
- Orchestrating multidisciplinary care
- Coordinating discharge planning
- Managing resource allocation
- Arranging follow-up care
- Coordinating with external providers

## 10. Evaluating
- Assessing treatment responses
- Measuring care quality metrics
- Analyzing patient outcomes
- Evaluating care plan effectiveness
- Reviewing intervention success
- Assessing goal achievement
- Conducting performance reviews
